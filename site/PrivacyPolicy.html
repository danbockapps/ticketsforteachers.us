Tickets for Teachers Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 8, 2026

Tickets for Teachers, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Tickets for Teachers program and the associated mobile messaging. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you opt into our text messaging program.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign up for the Tickets for Teachers mobile messaging, we collect the following information:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • Your mobile phone number.
  • Your preferences on event types.
  • Your opt-in consent record, including the date, time, and method of your consent.
  • Records of our mobile messaging communications with you, including delivery status and any responses you send (such as "HELP" or "STOP").

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your mobile phone number and associated information solely to:

  • Send you automated text alerts regarding last-minute ticket donations, event availability, and claiming instructions.
  • Process your requests to opt-in or opt-out of the mobile messaging.
  • Respond to your direct inquiries (e.g., if you text us for help).
  • Maintain compliance with applicable mobile messaging marketing laws and carrier regulations.

3. Explicit Prohibition on Third-Party Data Sharing

We value your trust and strictly protect your mobile data. No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes.

Your personal information, including your phone number and mobile messaging opt-in consent status, will not be sold, rented, leased, traded, or otherwise transferred to any third party. The only exceptions to this rule are:

Service Providers: We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers (such as our mobile messaging gateway platform) strictly for the purpose of transmitting our text messages to you. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and cannot use it for their own purposes.

Ticket Providers/Venues: Several partners of Tickets for Teachers require the release of name and contact information in exchange for tickets.

Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental regulation.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Your Choices and Opt-Out

You have complete control over your participation in the mobile messaging. You may opt out of receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us. Upon receiving a "STOP" request, we will send one final confirmation message and then cease all further mobile messaging communication unless you re-enroll.
